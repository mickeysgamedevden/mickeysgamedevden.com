Growing up in Switzerland in the 1970s and 1980s, I was captured early on by digital watches, Game & Watch handheld games, home computers, video game consoles and personal computers. It was natural for me to work with computers, and after a start as first level pc-supporter, I got a job as analyst/programmer. After several years of programming experience, mostly with the Java programming language, I started developing video games.

This place is my outlet to share ideas and thoughts about tools and technologies related to video game development.

Welcome to Mickey's Game-Dev-Den!
